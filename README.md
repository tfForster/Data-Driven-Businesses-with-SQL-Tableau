# Data-Driven-Businesses-with-SQL-Tableau

## Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the WBS Data Science Bootcamp.

The goal was to evaluate whether **Magist** is a suitable partner for **Eniac**, a company selling high-end tech products, by analyzing:
- product assortment and sales
- seller performance
- delivery times and delays

The analysis focuses on answering concrete business questions using **SQL**, visualizing results in **Tableau**, and summarizing insights in a presentation.

---

## Business Questions
The project addresses the following key areas:

### Products
- What tech product categories exist in Magist?
- How many tech products were sold and what share do they represent?
- What is the average product price?
- Are expensive tech products popular?

### Sellers
- How many months of data are available?
- How many sellers are there and how many are tech sellers?
- What share of sellers are tech sellers?
- What is the total and average monthly revenue of sellers (overall vs tech)?

### Delivery
- What is the average delivery time?
- How many orders are delivered on time vs delayed?
- Are there patterns in delayed deliveries (e.g. product size)?

---

## Tools Used
- **SQL (MySQL Workbench)** – data extraction and analysis
- **Tableau** – data visualization and dashboards
- **Google Slides / PDF** – final presentation of insights

---

## Project Workflow
1. Answered business questions using SQL queries  
2. Imported and analyzed data in Tableau  
3. Created visualizations to support insights  
4. Compiled findings into a business presentation  

---

## Project Files
- SQL scripts for analysis  
- Tableau workbook with visualizations  
- Final presentation summarizing results and recommendations  

---

## Conclusion
The analysis shows that while Magist offers access to the Brazilian market, limitations in scalability, delivery performance, and product-market fit raise concerns for high-end tech products. Alternative platforms may better support Eniac’s growth strategy.
